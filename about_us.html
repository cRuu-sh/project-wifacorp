Our Story

WIFA Corp has been established since 1992. Our company operates in several business sectors, including the procurement of goods and construction services, while also being actively involved in the healthcare sector. With over three decades of experience, we are committed to providing the best solutions for our partners.

1992

Established as CV. Andika, specializing in the procurement of goods and construction services.

1999

Launch of CV. Ilham Nusantara to broaden service capacity within the infrastructure sector.

2008

CV. Andika officially transformed into PT. Andika Karya Sejahtera.

2011

CV. Ilham Nusantara transitioned into PT. Ilham Nusantara, marking a significant scale-up in corporate operations.

Present

Operating as WIFA Corp, a trusted partner for major institutions like PT. Timah Tbk, Angkasa Pura II, and various government agencies.
